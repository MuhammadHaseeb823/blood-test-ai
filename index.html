A rule-based clinical reasoning engine designed to analyze laboratory parameters and produce structured educational findings, severity classification, suggestions, summaries and clinical explanations.

About Blood Test AI

Blood Test AI analyzes laboratory values using a rule-based reasoning system. Multiple abnormal findings can be identified from a single patient dataset.

The system is intended for educational and demonstration purposes. It does not provide a medical diagnosis.

Educational use only. Results require professional clinical interpretation and appropriate confirmatory testing.

How the System Works

  1. Patient information is entered.
  2. Laboratory values are entered.
  3. The rule-based engine evaluates the values.
  4. Potential findings are identified.
  5. Findings are assigned severity levels.
  6. Suggestions and educational explanations are displayed.
